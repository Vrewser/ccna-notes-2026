# Threat Actors

---

## Hacker Types

1. **White Hat Hackers**
    * Ethical hackers who use programming skills for good, ethical, and legal puposes.
    * They help in network penetration tests to find vulnerabilities.
2. **Gray Hat Hackers**
    * Individuals who commit crimes and do arguably unethical things, but not for personal gain or to cause damage.
    * They may disclose a vulnerability to the company after compromising their network.
3. **Black Hat Hackers**
    * Unethical criminals who compromise computers and networks for personal gain, or malicious intent, like network attacks.

### Hacking Terms:
1. **Script Kiddies**
    * Teenagers or inexperienced hackers running existing scripts, tools and exploits, to cause harm, but typically ot for profit.
2. **Vulnerability Broker**
    * Usually gray hat hackers that attempts to discover exploits of a network then report them to vendors, sometimes for a price or reward.
    * i.e `Google Vulnerability Reward Program (VRP)`
3. **Hacktivists**
    * Gray hat hackers that publicly protest organizations or governments by posting articles, videos, or leaking information attacks.
4. **Cyber criminals**
    * Black hat hackers that are either self-employed or working for a large cybercrime organization.
5. **State-sponsored**
    * White hat or black hat hackers.
    * They steal government secrets, gather intelligence, and sabotage networks.
    * The targets consists of foreign governments, terrorist groups, and corporations.

## State-sponsored Hackers
* Create advanced, customized attack code, usually using previously undiscovered software vulnerabilties called zero-day vulnerabilities.

**Zero-day vulnerability**
* An unknown software or hardware security flaw that developers have had zero days to fix before malicious actors exploit it.
